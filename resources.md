# Best job boards to use

Other English-friendly places to look besides this list.

## 1. [English Jobs in Germany](https://www.englishjobsingermany.com/)

Best match for HR and finance. Has dedicated HR & People and Finance & Accounting categories, plus location filters. Listings are presented as verified English-only roles.

## 2. [Jobs without German](https://www.jobswithoutgerman.de/)

Collects vacancies in Germany that do not ask for German, including People & HR, Finance & Legal, and Operations. Each listing links to the employer.

## 3. [EnglishJobs.de](https://www.englishjobs.de/)

A broad English-speaking job search engine with listings across Germany and regional filters. Useful for business roles and for comparing cities.

## 4. [Arbeitnow](https://www.arbeitnow.com/english-speaking-jobs)

Useful for startup and international-company jobs. Search English-language roles and look for visa sponsorship or relocation support. Check each employer's actual language requirements.

## 5. [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job/looking-for-job)

The official German government job portal for international skilled professionals. It has business, accounting, and administration categories. Inspect individual listings to see whether German is required.
